It is reported for transparency and is not used to replace any campaign label, retrain a surrogate, or support a mesh-convergence claim. ``Active-by-log'' excludes a nodal peak recorded after the associated FSP node had been removed; it is a diagnostic quantity, not a validated response label.

The isolations do not form a stable, monotonic refinement sequence. Shield-only refinement exceeded the pre-specified 5\% energy-deviation screening gate. FSP-only refinement suffered rapid FSP erosion, making its late raw nodal peak nonphysical for the stated numerical interpretation. The coupled M1 configuration was energetically stable but materially different from the frozen campaign response. Therefore, the diagnostic identifies a model-development requirement for a future mesh study, rather than a basis for extrapolating a mesh-independent penetration value.
