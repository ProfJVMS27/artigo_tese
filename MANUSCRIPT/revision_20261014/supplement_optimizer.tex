\section*{S8. Equal-budget optimizer benchmark}
The additional study comprises 100 runs and 6,030,000 candidate evaluations, using frozen dose and penetration pipelines. It is distinct from the original NSGA-II campaign used to select the thirty solver-re-evaluated configurations. Each condition uses ten paired seeds, 300 retained designs, and 60,300 evaluations per run. The lower limits are decision-threshold sensitivities only.

The main text reports aggregate results. Table~\ref{tab:s_optimizer_runs} preserves all per-run outcomes. HV uses the fixed DoE anchors without clipping coordinates better than the DoE ideal. IGD and generalized spread use the pooled empirical nondominated reference for each limit/margin combination. At 60 mm, the margin reference pools three methods, whereas the point-only reference contains NSGA-II only. Consequently, identical NSGA-II fronts can have different IGD values in those two columns of the protocol. Such differences are reference-set effects, not different solutions.

The spread of timings includes execution-condition variability and interruptions. The recorded sum is approximately 27.04 h, excluding time between stopped processes and work lost after the last checkpoint. It is not a controlled processor-time comparison, so these records do not establish that one optimizer is intrinsically faster.
