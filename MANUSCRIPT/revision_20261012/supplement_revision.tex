\section*{S6. Reproduction and boundaries of the revision}

The revision scripts are \path{audit_coauthor_revision.py}, \path{revision_extra_diagnostics.py}, and \path{build_revision_assets.py}. They read the archived Phase 2--5 evidence and write only the revision directory. The main audit records SHA256 hashes of its input CSVs. The MCNP inventory records output hashes. Test bootstrap resampling uses seed 20261012. Lightweight baselines use training-only five-fold CV with seed 42. The MCNP-filter sensitivity clones the frozen Ridge pipeline and changes training eligibility only. It does not overwrite the serialized operational model or reselect its hyperparameters. The subsequently completed equal-budget optimizer study is documented in Section S8. Nested/repeated development splits, mesh convergence, and additional time extensions are not reported as completed analyses.
